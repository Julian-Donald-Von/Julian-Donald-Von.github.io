About this edition

The public worldbook omits passages revealing later plot, character outcomes and private author material. Black bars are empty markers; the omitted original text is not included in the website or search index.

The English edition currently contains a world introduction, twenty-six regional overviews, maps and complete translations of the published novel chapters. The full gazetteers remain available in Chinese. A full-length machine translation was checked but withheld because of errors in quantities, technical terms and negation.

Map names use established English equivalents where available and romanisation elsewhere. The novel includes the prologue and Chapters 1–3 of Volume I in complete Chinese and English versions. Selected chapter illustrations and captions appear after the text and before reader comments. Audio, retired SVG extras and private author materials are not part of this release.
