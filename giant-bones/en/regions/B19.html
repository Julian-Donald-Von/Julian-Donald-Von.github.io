Member state · B19

Greywall Mining State

The southern right upper-arm region and the valleys between the forearm bones support mining, manufacturing and settled agricultural communities. Grey-brown brick, pale window bands and drained industrial housing courts answer local conditions.

Industrial towns and occupational representatives share political institutions. Materials, chemical processing, ceramic filtration and repair trades are important, but the state also has ordinary farms and civilian neighbourhoods. Industrial prosperity depends on water, transport, maintenance and labour.
