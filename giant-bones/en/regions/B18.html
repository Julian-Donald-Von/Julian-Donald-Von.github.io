Black Anvil Domain

Old right-shoulder routes lead into mining towns along the northern and middle right upper-arm region. Dark stone bases, red-brick walls and sawtooth factory roofs connect industrial buildings to their settlements.

A hereditary mining domain works with municipal financial representatives. Extraction, smelting, recycling and machine repair support miners' households and ordinary services. A civic anniversary belongs to the territory, not simply to the founding of a company.
