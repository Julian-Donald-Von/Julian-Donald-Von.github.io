Member state · B08

Nanyuan Transport Territory

Southern railway corridors, parts of the ankle and foot regions, farmland and the Nuanxi port territory belong to a large transport-oriented member. It does not own every stretch of the lower legs. Thick walls, deep eaves, ventilated courts and layered station streets suit its settlements.

A territorial assembly joins towns with different transport and agricultural interests. Railways, coastal shipping, border services and farming all matter. Stations, civilian homes, harbour works and managed facilities beyond the boundary must be distinguished from one another.
