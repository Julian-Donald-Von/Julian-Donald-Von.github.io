Tidegate Port State

Lake bays near the right hip, the Tidegate river valley and some sacral-side sluices form a port state. Pale ochre masonry, dark timber galleries, stepped banks and small public roofs suit communities living at different water levels.

Harbour transport, storage, boatbuilding and grain trades sustain city and countryside. Port councillors and reservoir-area towns must negotiate maintenance, water use and supply. Administrative boundaries and the shoreline do not coincide automatically.
