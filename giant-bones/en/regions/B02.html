Amber Bay Free City

Guilds and civic deliberation govern a compact commercial and craft centre on the western mandibular arc and its neighbouring shores. Terracotta brick, timber, deep eaves and continuous shopfronts define its streets.

Weighing instruments, woodwork, pottery, food and roadside hospitality connect the capital to smaller towns. Market inspection and public demonstrations of craft support civic pride; guild fees and sponsorship requirements can also exclude apprentices and newcomers.
