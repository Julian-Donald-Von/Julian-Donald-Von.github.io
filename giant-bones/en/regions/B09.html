Frostgate Marquisate

Pass towns follow the valleys and northern roads around the upper cervical region. Grey stone, pitched roofs, deep gateways and timber galleries give settlements a recognisable cold-season character.

A hereditary marquisate works through town charters. Road maintenance, forestry, livestock transport and boarding schools sustain the region. Low settlements connect to higher railway alignments by actual slopes and approaches; a station label does not erase the difference in elevation.
