Member state · B21

Amberlan Free State

The southern left wrist, palm-side lake bays and several finger routes form a commercial state with interlocking territories beside Elmport. Honey-coloured walls, grey-green eaves, warehouse galleries and blue-glazed water signs link its towns visually.

Internal shipping, repair, craft industries and lake-and-pond agriculture sustain the state. Port-city and branch-route town councils have distinct responsibilities. The Amberlan commercial compact is an institution, not another name for the member state itself.
