Elmport Republic

Forearm valleys, the northern left wrist and some workshop towns near the thumb form a republic of elected municipal representatives, with residence and property thresholds still restricting participation. Pale brick, elm-coloured timber and repeated glazed window grids establish its urban language.

Machine tools, clocks, instruments, vehicles and vocational schools sustain skilled work. Workshops depend on materials, apprenticeships, calibration and repair networks. The region is more than a catalogue of luxury instruments: working families and food supplies keep its towns functioning.
