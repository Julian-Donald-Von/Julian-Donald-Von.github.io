Peylan Autonomous State

The developed areas around the left third and fourth ribs and their river valleys support a civic and cultural state. Warm grey walls, blue tiles, slender eaves and theatre arcades create a consistent urban character.

Municipal and rural representatives share government. Theatre companies, printing, textiles, schools and farms sustain life beyond the main stages. Instrument-making, repair and touring networks connect performers to skilled trades and smaller settlements.
