Whitefurnace Industrial Domain

The left-shoulder transport gateway and the main industrial belt of the left upper arm support a varied industrial society. Warm red brick, large windows and restrained factory courtyards establish its architectural language.

Metallurgy, locomotives and boilers employ workers alongside services, food trades and ordinary manufacturing. Factory interests, urban representatives and a territorial administration share government. The domain contains working families and independent towns rather than forming one company's private country.
