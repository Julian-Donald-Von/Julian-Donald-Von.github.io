Holy Elm Chapterlands

A chapter administration, local town councils and occupational seats govern several historically connected tracts. Pale grey stone, varied but harmonious roof profiles and extensive courtyards shape its towns. Citizenship does not require religious belief.

Farming, boarding houses, printing, nursing and funeral services form a mixed economy. Institutional property and care work connect the region's public identity to ordinary livelihoods. Rural households and smaller towns remain part of its social landscape.
