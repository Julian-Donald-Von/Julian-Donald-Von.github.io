Special territory · J

Central High Plateau Common District

This jointly managed territory is not a twenty-fifth member state. Its institutions derive their responsibilities from shared agreements, while settlements and services still require ordinary administration, supply and maintenance.

The Common District has its own territorial record. Its residents, workplaces and public facilities should be read in that record rather than assigned automatically to the crownlands or treated as an empty space between states.
