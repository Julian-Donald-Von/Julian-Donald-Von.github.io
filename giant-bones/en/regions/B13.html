Member state · B13

Goldgrain Mercantile State

Bone terraces around the left fifth and sixth ribs meet the western Lingyuan plain and lands north of the seventh rib. Pale yellow brick, bronze railings, broad arcades and low roofs express its commercial prosperity.

Finance, textiles, storage, transport, agriculture and consumer markets support property-based representation. Wealth depends on land, credit, trade and labour rather than appearing solely as decoration in rich districts. Smaller market towns remain essential to the capital's supply.
