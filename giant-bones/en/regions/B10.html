Oakpass Commune State

Village and town communes occupy the lower cervical valleys and the northern approaches to the clavicular gateways. Deep timber eaves, pale walls and linked public courtyards carry a common local vocabulary.

Forestry, water-powered workshops, postal transport and communal landholding connect its settlements. A commune is a unit of government, not automatically a separate town. Water power remains seasonal and local rather than implying a universal electrical grid.
