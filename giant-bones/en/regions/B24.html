Member state · B24

Wheatplain Autonomous Territory

The left femoral region, Jiashi plateau and agricultural lands near the northern left tibia support a large, mature network of towns. Pale yellow stone, dark brown timber, gentle roofs and terrace-following arcades provide a common architectural vocabulary.

Mining, resource railways, grain and dairy farming sustain a mixed economy. Territorial interests, industrial towns and rural communes share an assembly. Extensive land contains settled farmland and service towns rather than remaining empty simply because the capital is far away.
