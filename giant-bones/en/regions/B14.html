Weyning Earldom

Historically connected lands around the right first and third ribs and old southern shoulder roads form an earldom with towns that retain budgetary powers. Grey-green stone, dark red roofs and low memorial courts give public spaces a restrained identity.

Wooded valleys, horticulture, historical education and the materials trade sustain both towns and countryside. The inherited territory does not coincide exactly with individual bones; some neighbouring tracts belong to other states.
