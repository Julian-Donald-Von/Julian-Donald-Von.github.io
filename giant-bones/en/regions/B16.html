Ulan Federation of Communes

Towns and rural communes cooperate across parts of the right seventh-to-tenth rib terraces, adjoining valleys and a freight district. Grey-ochre brick, shallow roofs and short courtyard galleries form a shared building language.

Craft manufacturing, wetland agriculture, inns and freight services link settlements of different sizes. Towns and rural communities have their own interests within the joint assembly; cooperation does not eliminate local disputes over revenue, water or transport.
