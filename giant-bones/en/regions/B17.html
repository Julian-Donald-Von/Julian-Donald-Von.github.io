Member state · B17

Crimson Cape Autonomous Territory

Orchards and trading roads connect lands beyond part of the left seventh rib with the developed areas around the eighth to tenth ribs. Ochre-pink plaster, blue-grey tiles, low orchard walls and hillside galleries give towns a warm, coherent appearance.

Fruit, wine-making, agricultural machinery, schools and southern commerce support the territory. Growers, cellars and merchants occupy different places in the supply chain. Renowned estates coexist with everyday producers and ordinary households.
