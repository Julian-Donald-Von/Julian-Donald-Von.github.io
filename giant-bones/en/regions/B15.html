Member state · B15

Silvermere Autonomous State

Towns, spring valleys and parts of the right fourth-to-sixth rib region share a landscape of cream walls, grey-blue eaves and long convalescent-style galleries. A taxpayer assembly operates alongside town representation.

Healthcare, horticulture, ordinary manufacturing and river-valley commerce coexist. Medical institutions require staff, supplies and transport, and their presence does not give every resident equal access to care. Gardens and working land support everyday life outside the main centres.
