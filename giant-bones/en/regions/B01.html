Member state · B01

Severen Crownlands

The royal capital and its sheltered university districts share a realm with forest towns, nurseries, farms and ordinary working neighbourhoods. Hereditary government coexists with specialised public offices. Bone-white masonry, dark blue roofs, colonnades and fine metalwork give its public buildings a coherent character.

Universities, finance, publishing, instruments and skilled services sustain the capital. Courtly consumption creates real employment, while scholarships, housing costs and patronage shape access to education. The woodland and farming hinterland supplies timber and food alongside imports from other member states.
