Tibo Free City

Tibo's urban belt occupies the right-wrist region and adjoining palm-edge settlements, with northern finger routes linking smaller places. Its capital is an inland railway trading centre; its name does not make it a seaport.

Chambers of commerce and district civic representatives share government. Warehouses, transport, craft workshops, retailers and ordinary neighbourhood services connect merchants to working households. Distant goods move through particular trading channels rather than an unrestricted global market.
