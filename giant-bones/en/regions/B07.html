Vilsa Pastoral Domain

Pastoral terraces, grain valleys and market towns support a society that combines herding with settled agriculture. Low white-stone walls, dark timber galleries and sheltered courtyards suit its exposed landscapes.

Dairy produce, wool, fine animal fibres, textiles and harness-making have distinct production chains and quality grades. Seasonal livestock work shapes household rhythms and the timing of local celebrations. Towns provide schools, repair trades and markets as well as services to herders.
